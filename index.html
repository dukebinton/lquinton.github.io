I'm a PhD student in the philosophy department at Rutgers (New Brunswick). My research interests are in the philosophy of mind and its intersections with cognitive science, philosophy of language, and social philosophy.

Research

A through line of my research is the relation between perception and conceptual thought. In particular, I'm interested in the natures of conception and perception, the mental processes in which concepts are deployed, and how we use concepts to coordinate with one another. I'm also interested in finding inroads from outside the contemporary analytic canon and tend to look for insights from philosophers in the existential/phenomenological (e.g. Heidegger, Kierkegaard, Beauvoir) and pragmatist (e.g. James, Sellars, McDowell) traditions.

At a more general level, my research has two "strands". The first strand starts by examining the resources the mind has at its disposal (e.g. concepts, attention, mental representation, memory) and considering how these resources bear on higher-order normatively-laden topics (e.g. competence, shared understanding, responsibility, justification). The second strand starts by observing some broadly social phenomena (e.g. coercion, identity, virtue) and trying to understand how best to frame these phenomena in terms internal to the mind (e.g. dispositions, belief fragmentation, mental transitions, perceptual skill).

About Me

Before coming to Rutgers, I graduated with an MA (2024) and BA (2022) in philosophy from San Francisco State University. In a previous life, I studied audio engineering at the Musician's Institute. I'm also a participant in talkPOPc, which organizes public-facing events about topics in philosophy and aesthetics.

When I'm not thinking about thinking, I aim for a normal distribution on Letterboxd, post music on my SoundCloud, and occassionally write for my Substack. My favorite albums are Big Thief's Dragon New Warm Mountain I Believe In You and Ryuichi Sakamoto's Playing the Piano. I'm a fan of the Boston Red Sox and (reluctantly) support Manchester United.
